Crosscheck: five marks

Five ways to mark a claim checked against something independent. Each is drawn for the quiet margins of a research record, shown at actual CSS heights of 24, 64 and 160 pixels.

Recommended: 2. The reference rule for its clear silhouette and relation to the site’s ruled evidence.
